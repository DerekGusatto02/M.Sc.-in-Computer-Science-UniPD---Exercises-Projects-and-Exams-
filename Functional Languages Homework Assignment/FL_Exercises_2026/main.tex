\section*{Instructions}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Instructions}

For your final assignment, you are required to solve the following exercises.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Solutions must be submitted as a PDF file generated using \LaTeX.
  \item Cooperation is allowed, but each student must submit their own solution.
  \item There is no strict deadline; however, to have the final grade registered
  in a given exam round, the homework must be submitted at least two weeks before
  the round date.
  \item Solutions must be sent to
  \href{mailto:francesco.gavazzo@unipd.it}{francesco.gavazzo@unipd.it}.
\end{itemize}

\section*{List of Exercises}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{List of Exercises}

All exercises refer to \href{https://www.scss.tcd.ie/matthew.hennessy/splexternal2015/resources/Pitts.pdf}{Pitt's notes}.

\begin{itemize}
  \item Exercises 2.5.1, 2.5.2, 2.5.3
  \item Two exercises of your choice among 3.4.4, 3.4.5, 3.4.6, 3.4.7
  \item Exercise 4.3.1
  \item Exercises 5.5.2, 5.5.3, 5.5.4 (bonus exercise)
\end{itemize}

In Exercises 5.5.2–5.5.4, it is allowed (but not mandatory) to restrict the
language to the pure $\lambda$-calculus, extended with integers and integer
operations. All semantic statements of the form
$\langle M, s \rangle \, R \, \langle M', s' \rangle$ must be replaced by
$M \, R \, M'$, as done in class.
